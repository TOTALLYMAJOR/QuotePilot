This is the single visual-review index for the approved QuotePilot direction and
the final interaction/IA closure frames. These are design artifacts only; they
do not indicate that the application has been implemented or deployed.

The governing behavior, route vocabulary, mobile utilities, and unsaved-change
rules are in [IMPLEMENTATION_CLOSURE.md](IMPLEMENTATION_CLOSURE.md).

ambiguities. No additional creative redesign is proposed here.
