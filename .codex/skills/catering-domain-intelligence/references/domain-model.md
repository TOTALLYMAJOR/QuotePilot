# Catering Domain Model

Last updated: 2026-09-08 15:04:45 CDT

Use this reference as a compact domain frame. It is advisory and must be
reconciled with current QuotePilot authority and code.

## Epistemic Classes

- `domain_fact`: broadly stable domain meaning supported by direct evidence.
- `domain_pattern`: recurring structure that may have tenant-specific variants.
- `failure_pattern`: a recurring way commercial or operational truth breaks.
- `design_implication`: a revisable product conclusion derived from a pattern.
- `quotepilot_doctrine`: a current repository-owned decision, named with its
  canonical source.
- `external_observation`: dated evidence about another system or operator.
- `hypothesis`: a plausible direction requiring validation.

Never present a `design_implication`, `external_observation`, or `hypothesis` as
universal catering law.

## Stable Domain Frame

- `domain_pattern`: Catering work continually translates customer intent into
  commercial agreement, operational preparation, execution, financial
  closeout, and reusable relationship knowledge.
- `domain_pattern`: One catering engagement can have simultaneous customer,
  commercial, operational, document, and evidence meanings. These meanings are
  connected but not interchangeable.
- `domain_pattern`: Important numbers require temporal meaning, provenance, and
  authority. Estimated, proposed, priced, accepted, planned, actual, and
  reconciled values are not synonyms.
- `domain_pattern`: Events change routinely. The product risk is not change
  itself but an unmanaged consequence, stale projection, or lost history.
- `domain_pattern`: Different actors need role-appropriate projections. A
  coherent workspace does not require one screen, document, or aggregate to own
  every concern.
- `design_implication`: Model stable references among commercial, payment,
  operational, document, and evidence authorities; do not create a giant event
  record merely to make the UI feel unified.

## Reconsideration Questions

For the provisional action, ask only the questions capable of changing it:

1. What real catering job and actor does this serve?
2. Which current value or transition is authoritative?
3. What commercial, operational, document, or workflow consequences follow?
4. Which audience may see or act on the result?
5. What evidence establishes success, ambiguity, staleness, or recovery?
6. Does the proposed method solve the user's objective at the correct layer?

The actor lists and object vocabulary in the source pack are discovery aids.
They do not create QuotePilot roles, schemas, lifecycle enums, or permissions.
