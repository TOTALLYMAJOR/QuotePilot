## Explicit Exclusions

- The pack's dated competitor intelligence is not part of automatic retrieval.
  Competitive tasks require current source verification before an observation
  may influence a decision.
- Illustrative money, staffing, margin, and count deltas are not policy.
- Actor and object inventories do not create QuotePilot roles, schemas, or
  permissions.
- The workflow atlas is not a runtime lifecycle enum.
- Generic pricing vocabulary does not establish supported QuotePilot rules.
- Recipes, procurement, rental availability, production quantities, and actual
  attendance remain advisory concepts unless current repository authority and
  executable evidence establish them.

## Conflict Rule

The knowledge layer cannot override current QuotePilot product behavior,
pricing, design, permission, release, field-state, or evidence authority. When
the domain model exposes a conflict with stronger authority, preserve the
stronger authority and report the conflict if it changes the work.
