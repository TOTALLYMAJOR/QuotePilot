## Final generation prompts

### `00-system-hero-generated.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: landscape portfolio case-study hero, 16:9
Primary request: create an editorial visual metaphor for turning operational ambiguity into a trustworthy product system
Scene/backdrop: warm ivory architectural workspace seen from a slightly elevated angle, with layered paper records, precise translucent glass channels, and a connected path moving from an unresolved cluster into a calm ordered operating system
Subject: a sequence of distinct but connected decision objects representing intake, quote, customer decision, payment evidence, and operations handoff; each remains visibly separate while sharing one coherent route
Style/medium: premium editorial still life, tactile paper and glass, understated systems-design aesthetic, realistic materials with subtle diagrammatic precision, not a software screenshot
Composition/framing: wide cinematic composition with a strong visual journey and generous negative space; suitable as a portfolio opening image
Lighting/mood: soft directional daylight, intelligent, calm, exacting, quietly ambitious
Color palette: warm ivory, near-black charcoal, burnished brass, muted moss green, restrained clay accents
Materials/textures: uncoated paper, smoked glass, fine brass edges, subtle graphite marks
Constraints: no people, no logos, no brands, no readable text, no fake UI, no watermark; make the separation of stages visually unmistakable without labels
Avoid: neon, generic tech gradients, floating holograms, glossy corporate stock imagery, clutter, fantasy machinery
```

### `01-human-led-ai-generated.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: landscape portfolio section image, 16:9
Primary request: create an editorial visual metaphor for human-led AI collaboration where AI accelerates discovery and synthesis but a human retains authority for consequential decisions
Scene/backdrop: a dark charcoal worktable transitioning into warm ivory, with many fine luminous threads of possibility being gathered into a small number of precise physical channels
Subject: one natural human hand calmly selecting and anchoring a brass decision token while translucent analytical forms and light traces remain advisory around it; the final action passes through a clear human-controlled gate
Style/medium: premium editorial photography blended with restrained conceptual art, tactile and credible, not science fiction
Composition/framing: wide horizontal composition, the hand and decision gate are the focal point, with negative space for adjacent portfolio copy
Lighting/mood: warm focused light, thoughtful, accountable, composed, high craft
Color palette: charcoal, warm ivory, burnished brass, muted moss green, a very small amount of amber light
Materials/textures: skin texture, uncoated paper, brushed metal, smoked glass, graphite notation without readable words
Constraints: no face, no logos, no brands, no readable text, no fake UI, no watermark; make the human decision visibly final and the surrounding intelligence visibly advisory
Avoid: robots, humanoid AI, brains, circuitry clichés, blue neon, cyberpunk, corporate handshake imagery, magical effects
```
