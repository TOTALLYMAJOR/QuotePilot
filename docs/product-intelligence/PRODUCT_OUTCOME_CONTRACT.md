# Product Outcome Contract

Last updated: 2026-09-16 23:25:23 CDT

Status: proposed product contract; grounded in current repository authority,
but numerical targets and outcome claims require owner acceptance and evidence.

## Product purpose

QuotePilot exists to help catering teams convert uncertain customer intent into
an explainable, profitable, and operationally supportable engagement while
preserving what was proposed, accepted, planned, executed, paid, and learned.

The product should reduce the human reconciliation work now performed across
memory, email, spreadsheets, payment tools, and disconnected documents. It may
compose those truths, but it may not collapse them into one mutable record or
claim that one evidence class proves another.

## Primary actors and jobs

| Actor | Job to be done | Value QuotePilot should add |
|---|---|---|
| Catering owner-operator | Decide which work is worth accepting and whether the business can deliver it profitably. | Faster, more defensible commercial decisions with fewer hidden cost, scope, and execution risks. |
| Sales or catalog administrator | Turn inquiry details and catalog choices into a current, accurate proposal. | Less duplicate entry, faster priced drafts, clear blockers, and controlled revisions. |
| Operations manager | Turn an accepted promise into current staffing, production, timing, rental, and document work. | Fewer late discoveries and stale plans; clearer ownership of the next action. |
| Kitchen or production lead | Prepare from the correct menu, quantity basis, cost evidence, and current BEO. | Less work from superseded or ambiguous instructions. |
| Staffing administrator or event lead | Plan and confirm the right people against the current event basis. | Fewer conflicts and stale recommendations without automatic assignment authority. |
| Finance or owner reviewer | Explain deposits, balances, payouts, cost evidence, and realized contribution. | Faster reconciliation and less unexplained leakage. |
| Customer or event contact | Understand the current offer, make a decision, supply changes, and know what is confirmed. | A clearer decision experience without exposure to private staff, margin, or provider evidence. |

## Outcome set

| Outcome ID | Intended improvement | Successful when | Must never be inferred from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OUT-01` Decision-ready inquiry | Relevant customer intent becomes a reviewable opportunity without inventing scope, price, availability, or consent. | Staff can identify the request, missing facts, drift, and next authorized action. | Submission count or model/provider configuration alone. |
| `OUT-02` Explainable commercial commitment | A priced quote and customer-current proposal are accurate, versioned, and understandable. | The accepted snapshot, changes, price, cost evidence, margin availability, and payment obligation remain explainable. | A draft, local calculation, later timestamp, or browser presentation alone. |
| `OUT-03` Commitment-to-execution continuity | Accepted work becomes current operational preparation without rewriting commercial history. | Staffing, production, Inventory, BEO, workflow, and timing consumers identify their exact basis, staleness, owner, and next action. | One blended readiness score or an accepted quote alone. |
| `OUT-04` Controlled execution and recovery | Operators can recognize what changed, what failed, and how to recover safely. | Ambiguous, stale, contradictory, failed, and completed states remain distinct and recovery preserves receipts and authority. | Acknowledgement, retry, or UI status without authoritative outcome evidence. |
| `OUT-05` Defensible financial closeout | Payments, costs, payouts, and contribution can be reconciled to the governed engagement. | Unexplained discrepancies and missing evidence are visible and operator action is bounded. | Provider dispatch, client success response, or synthetic fixtures. |
| `OUT-06` Reusable relationship learning | Completed work improves the next engagement without copying obsolete authority. | Rebooking and relationship context reuse safe facts while excluding signatures, payments, provider evidence, and stale prices. | Record cloning or anecdotal memory alone. |

## Success model

QuotePilot succeeds only when four conditions hold together:

1. **Value:** a named catering actor completes a meaningful job more safely,
   quickly, or clearly.
2. **Authority:** the result comes from the system that owns that truth.
3. **Quality:** security, pricing, reliability, accessibility, privacy, and
   evidence guardrails remain within bounds.
4. **Learning:** a release or experiment record allows the owner to adopt,
   revise, stop, or investigate based on evidence.

Usage is not value by itself. A click, session, created quote, or opened screen
is a leading signal until connected to a downstream decision or outcome.

## Product boundaries

- QuotePilot does not automatically establish physical availability,
  production completion, attendance, provider settlement, or human acceptance.
- Suggestions, model output, reconciliation findings, and simulations remain
  advisory until an existing trusted authority acts.
- Estimated, proposed, priced, accepted, planned, actual, and reconciled values
  remain separate.
- Customer views never expose private margin, staffing, provider, or internal
  evidence without an explicit current contract.
- Synthetic founder-pilot records are useful for source and workflow proof, not
  baselines, adoption, or commercial outcomes.

## Acceptance decision

The owner accepts this contract by approving the outcome set and the proposed
target policy in `BASELINES_AND_TARGETS.md`. Until then, it is the working
measurement hypothesis, not proof that these outcomes have occurred.
