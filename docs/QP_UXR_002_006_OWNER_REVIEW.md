## QP-UXR-002

### Before

The operator could see pending work, but had to reconstruct the event, customer,
commercial stake, source revision, dependency implications, evidence boundary,
and whether a visible approval meant the approved action had executed. After a
Workflow decision, they also had to find their way back and determine whether
the original obligation still existed.

### After

Clear the Deck presents one exact decision record in business terms, hands it to
the exact role-gated Workflow object, preserves the decision context beside the
control, and confirms an outcome only after matching authority readback. An
uncertain attempt freezes duplicate submission. Return context restores the
same decision or the source heading after fresh evidence proves it disappeared.

### Evidence

- Unit/component coverage exercises stale scope, role-safe eligibility,
  Decision Debt retention, request-bound proof, uncertain remount, source
  disappearance, and exact focus restoration.
- Browser evidence:
  `output/playwright/ambient-intelligence-current/clear-deck-decision-{390,768,1440}.png`.
- Source/local only: no connected provider outcome, hosted result, production
  mutation, or human acceptance is claimed.

## QP-UXR-005

### Before

Control Room exposed planning sections, but operators still had to decide which
facts were satisfied, which were blockers, which were merely unavailable, and
whether the next button actually addressed the Preflight conclusion. Duplicate
attention/evidence summaries made the narrow layout long and repetitive.

### After

Event Preflight is the dominant decision object. It separates **Ready /
satisfied facts**, **Needs attention**, and **Unknown / unavailable**; preserves
the reason and evidence source; and places one governed **Next** action directly

fenced to current, exact, decidable evidence. Missing receipt fields, stale
generations, wrong revisions, late reads, undated/invalid peers, missing timing,

event session, automatic staffing, automatic BEO, or generalized workflow
authority. This recommendation requires explicit owner approval and a new

## Owner Test Itinerary

Use an authenticated admin or sales workspace with three representative records:

- **Decision record:** a quote with one pending exact approval request.
- **Amendment record:** a sent/viewed, non-terminal quote whose current catalog
  and quote revision can be reviewed and safely amended.
- **Event record:** a booked event with an exact acceptance receipt, recorded
  deposit, unpaid final balance, incomplete final-count checklist, stale or
  unavailable BEO/staffing evidence, and a same-venue overlap.

1. **Now — `/app`**
   - Action: inspect the top commercial priority and open its exact action.
   - Expect: exact quote/event/customer identity, recorded value or explicit
     unavailable state, separate lifecycle/payment/booking facts, consequence,
     and one next action.
   - Proof: Workflow receives the same quote and obligation IDs.
   - Failure test: make the bounded read stale; mutation controls must withdraw.

2. **Clear the Deck — `/app/clear-the-deck`**
   - Action: open the pending approval in Workflow.
   - Expect: business stake, timing, source revision, dependency, evidence, and
     authority remain visible before action.
   - Proof: the action locator is bound to the exact request ID.
   - Failure test: remove/mismatch the request; review must fail closed.

3. **Workflow — `/app/workflow` with the exact query identity**
   - Action: resolve the request with an authorized role.
   - Expect: approval and approved-action execution remain distinct; pending,
     uncertain, reconciled, and confirmed states are explicit.
   - Proof: confirmation requires matching same-organization server readback.
   - Failure test: interrupt the client response; a second write must remain
     frozen while the original attempt reconciles.

4. **Return to Clear the Deck**
   - Action: use the normal return path after reconciliation.
   - Expect: the original decision regains focus, or the Clear the Deck heading
     does when a fresh read proves that obligation is gone.
   - Failure test: another decision must never be substituted.

5. **Governed amendment — `/app/quotes/<amendment-quote-id>/edit`**
   - Action: change guest count or another supported commercial field, review
     semantic differences and authoritative consequences, then apply.
   - Expect: current commitment, reversible proposed intent, semantic change,
     authoritative simulation, consequences, required authority, and a
     `Changed / Preserved / Needs attention / Next` receipt.
   - Proof: new revision identity and preserved prior evidence are explicit.
   - Failure test: change quote/catalog context after review; apply must require
     a fresh simulation. Interrupt apply and verify reconciliation before retry.

6. **Opportunities — `/app/quotes`**
   - Action: find the amended/current opportunity and follow its next action.
   - Expect: recorded commercial movement and exact evidence states appear
     without browser repricing or a synthetic score.
   - Proof: the next action remains bound to that quote after Workflow return.
   - Failure test: missing money must remain unavailable, not `$0` or unpaid.

7. **Events — `/app/events`**
   - Action: open the booked representative event.
   - Expect: accepted/booked commitment context appears without per-row BEO or
     staffing calls.
   - Proof: selected quote ID carries into Event Focus.
   - Failure test: an unknown event ID must show recovery, not another event.

8. **Event Focus — `/app/events/<event-quote-id>`**
   - Action: review the current commitment and enter Control Room.
   - Expect: saved scope, revision, plan, payment/acceptance context, and known
     planning gaps form one briefing.
   - Proof: Control Room retains the exact quote ID.

9. **Control Room / Event Preflight — `/app/events/<event-quote-id>/live`**
   - Action: inspect all three Preflight classes and use **Next**.
   - Expect: acceptance-revision mismatch, unpaid balance, final count, schedule
     overlap, BEO/staffing state, and unavailable inventory/attendance are not
     collapsed together. Next appears before run-of-show detail and opens the
     authority named by the displayed condition.
   - Proof: refreshed BEO/staffing reads match organization, quote, revision, and
     snapshot generation.
   - Failure test: use stale/partial data, malformed acceptance, wrong-revision
     authority results, an undated peer, or unknown guest load; no affected fact
     may pass.

10. **Replay — `/app/events/<event-quote-id>/replay`**
    - Action: inspect supporting evidence.
    - Expect: Replay remains unavailable as a complete execution history;
      current-record milestones are labeled as incomplete support.
    - Failure test: checklist timestamps must not become live phase, attendance,
      actuals, or completion.
