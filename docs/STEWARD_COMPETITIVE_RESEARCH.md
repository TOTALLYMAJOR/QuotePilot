# QuotePilot Steward Competitive Research

Last updated: 2026-08-20 14:47:39 CDT

Status: Evidence snapshot for product design
Date: August 15, 2026
Scope: Publicly described catering, venue, proposal, and client-workflow products

## Research question

What can QuotePilot offer as a paid AI setup and quoting assistant that is
materially more valuable than generic proposal generation while remaining safe
for the operator, the customer, and QuotePilot's commercial records?

## Method and limitation

This review uses public first-party product pages, help material, and product
announcements available on August 15, 2026. It describes public evidence, not
private roadmaps or exhaustive hands-on testing. "No public evidence found"
must not be interpreted as proof that a competitor has never considered or
built a capability.

## Executive finding

Fast proposals, reusable menus, connected event documents, AI writing, change
notifications, and revenue recommendations are no longer sufficient
differentiators. Tripleseat is the closest strategic threat because it publicly
describes AI-generated initial quotes and follow-ups, awareness of changed event
details, revenue intelligence, and future agentic products.

The strongest open position is a **proof-carrying commercial copilot**:

> QuotePilot Steward prepares the decision, proves the consequences, and shows
> who has authority. It never silently becomes the decision-maker.

The product artifact is not a chat answer. It is a Decision Packet containing
verified facts, proposed menu or quote changes, authoritative pricing and
margin evidence, operational consequences, assumptions, unresolved questions,
customer-response language, policy gates, and an expiring review boundary.

## Competitive whitespace

### 1. Consequence before change

Competitors commonly update connected documents after a detail changes.
Steward should answer a different question first: "If we do this, what else
becomes wrong or needs review?" It should compile a counterfactual across:

- price, taxes, fees, and deposits;
- margin only when cost evidence is complete;
- staffing requirements and coverage gaps;
- production artifacts and freshness;
- menu, allergen, dietary, capacity, and lead-time uncertainties; and
- customer commitments and approval requirements.

### 2. Proof-carrying suggestions

Every material field in a Decision Packet should carry one of these sources:

- exact customer or operator wording;
- approved tenant policy and revision;
- current catalog item and revision;
- authoritative pricing result;
- current quote/version receipt;
- deterministic operational rule; or
- explicitly labeled model suggestion with no authority.

Unsupported claims remain unknown. Confidence language must never substitute
for evidence.

### 3. Authority as a product feature

Most AI products warn users to review output. Steward should make review
executable. Every proposed action states who may stage it, who may save it, what
must be re-simulated, and what the system is prohibited from doing. The safe
path is part of the recommendation, not a disclaimer below it.

### 4. Safe refusal that still completes useful work

When Steward cannot safely price a custom item, verify an allergen claim, grant
a discount, or answer a legal question, it should not stop at "I can't help."
It should:

1. explain the missing authority or evidence;
2. prepare the safe portions of the work;
3. identify the smallest human decision required; and
4. provide the exact next question or review route.

### 5. A house playbook, not cross-customer imitation

Steward should learn only from approved organization policies, catalog data,
and tenant-owned accepted/booked history through deterministic aggregates. It
must not train on or expose another organization's pricing, customers, menu,
language, or outcomes.

## Proposed position

**Category:** Governed commercial copilot for catering

**Promise:** "Walk into the conversation with a second set of eyes. Walk out
with a quote you can explain."

**Trust line:** "Prepared by Steward. Verified by QuotePilot. Approved by you."

**Competitive claim that can be tested:** QuotePilot can reduce time from a
qualified inquiry to a review-ready quote while preserving exact provenance and
zero autonomous customer-impacting actions.

## What forms the moat

The moat is not the model or prompt. It is the combination of:

- QuotePilot's server-authoritative pricing;
- Commercial Change Authority and exact-revision receipts;
- tenant-specific catalog and policy evidence;
- customer request and quote-version traceability;
- an eval corpus of real catering edge cases without cross-tenant leakage;
- a visible authority contract operators learn to trust; and
- outcome telemetry that measures accepted recommendations, corrections,
  blocked unsafe actions, quote speed, and downstream rework separately.

## Product warning

Tripleseat's public direction overlaps with quote generation, change awareness,
revenue intelligence, and agentic workflows. QuotePilot should not market
"AI for catering" as the innovation. It should ship the narrower and harder
claim: **commercial intelligence with receipts and bounded authority**.
